The Texas Advantage: What DeSoto Sellers Should Know

Texas offers several unique benefits for homeowners, but also has specific considerations when selling property:

Texas Homestead Exemption

If you've been claiming the homestead exemption on your DeSoto property, you've enjoyed reduced property tax liability. When selling, understanding how this affects your timeline and proceeds is important. Cash sales typically close faster, allowing you to transition smoothly to your next residence and potentially claim the exemption there sooner.

Community Property Laws

Texas is a community property state, which means property acquired during marriage generally belongs to both spouses equally. If you're selling due to divorce, working with a cash buyer who can close quickly once both parties agree can simplify a complicated process.

No State Income Tax

One advantage of selling your home in Texas is that you won't pay state income tax on the proceeds. Combined with federal capital gains exclusions for primary residences, many DeSoto homeowners keep more of their equity when they sell.

Texas Foreclosure Timeline

Texas is known for having one of the fastest foreclosure processes in the nation, with non-judicial foreclosures potentially completed in as little as 27 days after the notice is filed. If you're facing foreclosure on your DeSoto home, time is critical. Selling to a cash buyer can help you avoid foreclosure, protect your credit, and potentially walk away with money in your pocket.
