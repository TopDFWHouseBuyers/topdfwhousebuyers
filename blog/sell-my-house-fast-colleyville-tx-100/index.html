Texas-Specific Considerations When Selling Your Home

Selling a house in Texas comes with unique considerations that homeowners in other states don't face. Understanding these Texas-specific factors can help you make informed decisions about your property sale.

Texas Homestead Exemption

Texas offers generous homestead exemptions that protect homeowners from certain creditors and provide property tax benefits. When you sell your homestead property in Colleyville, you'll want to understand how this affects your tax situation and whether you'll be establishing a new homestead. The good news is that Texas has no state income tax, so you won't pay state capital gains taxes on your home sale.

Community Property Laws

Texas is a community property state, which means that if you're married, your spouse typically has an ownership interest in your home even if only your name is on the deed. Both spouses usually need to sign off on the sale of a homestead property. We handle these situations regularly and can guide you through the process smoothly.

Texas Foreclosure Timeline

If you're behind on mortgage payments in Colleyville, understanding Texas foreclosure laws is critical. Texas is primarily a non-judicial foreclosure state, meaning the process can move relatively quickly compared to other states. From the first missed payment to foreclosure sale can be as little as 120-150 days. If you're facing foreclosure, selling to a cash buyer can help you avoid the foreclosure on your record and potentially walk away with cash instead of nothing.
