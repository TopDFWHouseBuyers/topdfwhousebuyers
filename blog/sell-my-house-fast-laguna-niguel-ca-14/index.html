California-Specific Considerations When Selling As-Is

Understanding California's Disclosure Requirements

Even when selling as-is in California, sellers must still provide required disclosures about the property's condition. This includes the Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) and Natural Hazard Disclosure. The good news? When working with an experienced cash buyer like Golden Coast Cash Offer, we guide you through these requirements and accept the property in its current state—disclosures and all.

Navigating Tenant-Occupied Properties

California has some of the nation's strongest tenant protection laws, making it challenging to sell occupied rental properties on the traditional market. Many conventional buyers won't consider tenant-occupied homes, fearing complications with California's eviction restrictions. Cash buyers, however, regularly purchase tenant-occupied properties, allowing you to sell without navigating the complex process of tenant relocation.

Property Tax Considerations

Thanks to Proposition 19, California sellers over 55 may be eligible to transfer their property tax base to a new home, providing significant savings in our high-tax state. When you sell quickly for cash, you can time your sale strategically to maximize these benefits while minimizing your carrying costs.
