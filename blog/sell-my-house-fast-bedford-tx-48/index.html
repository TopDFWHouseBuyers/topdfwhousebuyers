Selling a rental property through a real estate agent in Bedford presents unique challenges. First, you need to coordinate showings around tenant schedules, which can take months. Second, most buyers want move-in ready homes, but your rental may need updates after years of tenant wear and tear.

Agent commissions typically cost 6% of the sale price, plus you will likely need to make repairs that appraisers or inspectors identify. For a rental property in Bedford valued at $300,000, that could mean $18,000 in commissions alone, not counting repairs, staging, or the carrying costs while the property sits on the market. Meanwhile, you are still responsible for property taxes, insurance, and any issues that arise.
