Selling a House With Foundation Problems in Texas

Published June 13, 2026 · Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas

Foundation problems are among the most stressful issues a homeowner can face, especially when you need to sell. If you are dealing with foundation damage in the Dallas-Fort Worth area, you are probably wondering about your legal obligations, potential repair costs, and whether anyone will even buy your home.

Understanding Foundation Issues in North Texas

The Dallas-Fort Worth area sits on expansive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks during dry periods. This constant movement puts tremendous pressure on foundations, causing cracks, settling, and structural damage. North Texas homeowners are particularly vulnerable due to our extreme weather cycles and periodic droughts.

Common foundation problems in DFW include horizontal or vertical cracks, uneven floors, doors that stick, gaps between walls and ceilings, and moisture intrusion. These issues can range from minor cosmetic concerns to serious structural damage requiring immediate attention.

Texas Disclosure Laws You Must Know

Texas law requires sellers to complete a Seller Disclosure Notice for most residential transactions. You must disclose any known foundation problems, previous repairs, or structural issues. Failure to disclose can result in legal liability even after the sale closes.

The Texas Property Code specifically addresses material defects that could affect a buyer's decision to purchase. Foundation problems definitely qualify. Attempting to hide these issues is not only unethical but can lead to expensive lawsuits down the road. Honesty is always the best policy when selling house foundation problems Texas homeowners face.

The True Cost of Foundation Repair in DFW

Foundation repair costs in Texas vary dramatically based on severity and repair method. Minor crack repairs might cost $500 to $2,000, while extensive repairs requiring piers or underpinning can range from $5,000 to $40,000 or more. Most DFW foundation repairs average between $8,000 and $15,000.

Getting multiple quotes from licensed foundation repair companies is essential. However, many homeowners discover that investing tens of thousands into repairs does not guarantee they will recoup those costs when selling. The property's value may not increase enough to justify the expense.

Your Options for Selling With Foundation Problems

You have several paths forward when selling a house with foundation issues in Texas. First, you could pay for repairs yourself before listing. This makes the home more marketable but requires significant upfront investment with no guaranteed return.

Second, you could list the property as-is and price it below market value to account for needed repairs. Traditional buyers will likely struggle to get financing, as most lenders will not approve loans for homes with significant foundation problems. This severely limits your buyer pool.

Third, you could sell directly to a cash buyer who specializes in properties needing repairs. Cash buyers purchase homes in any condition, handle all repairs themselves, and can close quickly without financing contingencies.
